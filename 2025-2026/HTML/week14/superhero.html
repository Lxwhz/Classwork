Ben was once the perfect hero a boy gifted with reality warping, time manipulation, fate control, and absolute immortality, who used tools like his times stop watch, law-binding chains, marked arrows, and paradox gauntlet to save everyone through abilities like Existence Rewrite, Rewind State, Future Step, Guaranteed Outcome, Infinite Regen, and Absolute State but as his mental limits fractured under endless timelines, paradoxes, and loops, and as eternal boredom and isolation consumed him, he came to believe the world’s suffering was caused by free will itself, ultimately transforming into a villain who no longer saves reality, but controls it completely so nothing can ever go wrong again.

Julian Vane was a judge in the corrupt city of New Essex, obsessed with the "letter of the law." During a city-wide riot fueled by a mob boss he was about to convict, the courthouse was stormed. Faced with total anarchy, Vane’s rigid mind snapped—and bonded with a metaphysical force of Order. In that moment, he didn't just represent the law; he became its avatar. He manifested the Gavel of Gravity, subdued the mob with a pulse of golden energy, and walked out of the ruins as Law. Now, he patrols the world as a living deterrent, unable to ignore a crime but physically bound by the very rules he enforces.
